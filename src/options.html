Sure! This box mirrors the look of an exported chat. Adjust the fonts, size, and toggles above to see them applied here in real time.

Connect your Notion workspace to export chats as native Notion pages. the extension can only see and modify pages you explicitly grant access to.

New chat pages will be created as children of this page. To grant access to more pages: open the page in Notion → "..." menu → Connections → the extension.

Connect your Microsoft account to export chats as .docx files in Word Online. Files are stored in OneDrive under the the extension app folder.

Word Online exports appear in OneDrive under Apps → the extension. OneNote exports go to the default notebook's Quick Notes section.

Save chats to your own Nextcloud server via WebDAV. the extension uses an app password (Nextcloud → Settings → Security → "Create new app password") - your real account password never leaves your browser.
